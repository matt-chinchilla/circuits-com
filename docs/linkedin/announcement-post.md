I would like to introduce you all to a company I have been building over the course of the last 5 months: Circuit Center

In a nutshell, it is a components directory for the electronics industry: search for parts, compare stock & pricing from distributors, and find the people who sell them. The goal is for it to become "the tool" from start to finish: imagine any PCB, build it in our web app, source the parts from a plethora of distributors, and eventually get it manufactured through a partner. I want Circuit Center to be where an engineer starts and finishes when sourcing a board. It is a big ambition, and a lot of work ahead (you can see it for yourself at circuitcenter.ai).

For right now, it is a place for price comparison and data analytics across our 28 categories and 189 subcategories, and that catalog is actively growing as we speak. The data presently on the site is placeholder, as real pricing, stock, and listings will only come from our eventual partners, which will grow a great deal more with 3 of you.

I am actively looking for 3 companies that want free advertisement on the website, at the sole cost of working with us on fielding questions & information as this grows. Suppliers and distributors, manufacturers, individual salespeople looking to stand out at their company -> and anyone I have neglected to mention is welcome to apply or reach out anyway.

Nothing is set in stone yet, but there are 3 tiers of subscription (all with differing benefits), likely subject to price changes as the business grows. So here is what I can promise:

1) A permanent price-lock at the initial rate for whichever subscription you eventually want, for as long as you have a subscription with us.

2) $0 charged until Circuit Center reaches the stated KPIs we define success by, and only then will you be offered a chance to join, not "sneak-attacked" with some subscription you had believed was free up until that point.

3) A direct line of contact with me and one of our dedicated sales reps. All ideas are welcome, and your insights (if helpful!) go a long way toward making you grow as a business, because that is all this is really for anyway.

What I'm asking in return isn't money. It's a real company using it the way a real company does: your catalog, part numbers, your customers clicking through, and telling me where it falls short. We have grown so far through open source data gathering and some intelligent insights, but real partners would go an extremely long way toward accelerating the product :)

Critiques genuinely welcome, especially the blunt ones. That's worth more to me right now than the revenue is.

Interested, curious, or want to tell me what's wrong with it? Email matthew@circuitcenter.ai, comment below, or DM me.

Follow the page for what comes next.

## Editorial decisions, for the record

- Announcement-led, not offer-led. The company is the subject; the founding-partner offer is an
  invitation extended because the company is new.
- Promise 2 (no sneak-attack) is the strongest line in the post. Do not soften it.
- Placeholder-data disclosure is deliberately BROAD ("the data on the site today is placeholder")
  so it covers pricing and sponsor slots in one sentence.
- No fixed part count: the catalog is actively growing, so the post says so rather than dating
  itself with a number.
- Emoji removed at the author's request; the ":)" is his and stays.
- Hashtags: #SupplyChain retained for discovery, #CircuitCenter added for the daily-post cadence
  the author plans.

## Known limitation, accepted by the author (2026-08-18)

The placeholder disclosure lives in the POST, not on the site. A visitor arriving from search, a
forwarded link, or a category page months from now sees no such notice. Separately, the live
`sponsors` table shows Arrow Electronics, Digi-Key and Mouser as Platinum "Exclusive Partner"
rows, alongside demo/test rows. The author reviewed both points and chose to leave the data
as-is and cover it with the post's disclosure. Recorded here so the decision is not re-litigated
and so whoever reads this next knows it was deliberate rather than missed.
